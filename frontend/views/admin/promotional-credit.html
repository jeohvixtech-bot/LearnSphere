🎁 Promotional Credit

Non-withdrawable value granted to tutors. It offsets first match commission and nothing else — it can never be paid out in cash, and it expires 6 months after it is granted.

Credit is spent automatically: the moment a tutor is charged first-match commission, the oldest grant covers it and the same amount is returned to their withdrawable balance. There is no "apply" step.
