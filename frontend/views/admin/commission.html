First time enabling commission

Commission will apply only to invoices paid from the moment you save. Everything tutors have already earned stays untouched — enabling this will never claw back money that has already been credited to them.

Changing an active rate

Commission already charged keeps the rate it was charged at — past deductions are never rewritten. The new rate applies to invoices paid from now on.

How it works

  • When a parent's invoice is paid, the tutor is credited the full amount and the commission is deducted as a separate line on their ledger.
  • Both lines appear on the tutor's statement, so the deduction is visible rather than baked silently into a smaller number.
  • If an invoice is later refunded, the earning and its commission are both reversed — the platform keeps no cut of money the parent didn't ultimately pay.
  • Commission is only ever taken from withdrawable funds today. Once the credit bucket exists, credit will be consumed first.
