The monthly payout pipeline: cutoff → review → approve → bank transfer. A batch moves as a whole, so a month is never half-paid.

A tutor is only paid for sessions that were delivered and sit on a paid invoice, and never more than their ledger balance. A first tuition period therefore pays nothing unless promotional credit offset its commission.
